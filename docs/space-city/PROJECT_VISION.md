# Persistent Space City

Players inhabit individual characters in a persistent multiplayer space city alongside autonomous NPC citizens. This is a standalone SS14 fork, built on RobustToolbox and SS14's simulation. It is not a colony-management game.

## Principles

- A functioning society continues working, maintaining infrastructure, consuming resources, and socializing without player direction.
- Players participate in that society through their own characters: employment, relationships, services, construction, exploration, and cooperation.
- NPCs use the same real items, ingredients, machinery, access controls, health, and movement mechanics as players wherever practical. A cook obtains ingredients and operates kitchen equipment; a janitor uses tools and removes actual messes.
- World state has lasting consequences. Construction, supplies, injuries, citizen identity, and careers eventually survive server restarts.
- Reuse upstream functionality, preserve multiplayer correctness, and favor small independently verifiable milestones.

## Long-term scope

Humanoid citizens; needs and schedules; service, medical, engineering, cargo, and security work; infrastructure upkeep; player/NPC interactions; durable characters and resources; and station growth. Population scale and unattended operation require measurement rather than an assumed NPC capacity.

## Current boundary

Milestone 0 establishes the development environment and architectural evidence only. No citizen gameplay, economy, custom NPC framework, persistence implementation, or engine modification is introduced. See `ROADMAP.md` for subsequent milestones, each requiring approval before starting.
